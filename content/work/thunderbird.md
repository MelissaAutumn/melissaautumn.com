I joined Thunderbird near the end of 2022 as a Web Developer working on the web / infrastructure side of things. This included work on [thunderbird.net](https://github.com/thunderbird/thunderbird-website), [stats.thunderbird.net](https://github.com/thunderbird/stats), and [addons.thunderbird.net](https://github.com/thunderbird/addons-server/tree/master). 

After accidentally taking the lead on [Thunderbird Appointment](https://github.com/thunderbird/appointment) I was promoted several times, first to Senior Software Engineer under the newly formed Web Services team. After extensive work on Appointment, I noticed some areas where we could do better, and started several projects such as [Services UI](https://github.com/thunderbird/services-ui) where we coordinate Vue.js components and patterns for re-use throughout our services. 

Seeing a need to consolidate our account and soon-to-be subscription platform I helped architect [Thunderbird Accounts](https://github.com/thunderbird/thunderbird-accounts) which is the core connecting tissue of the Thundermail service. I was promoted to Staff Software Engineer soon after, and Thundermail was released in closed beta to subscribers in early 2026.
